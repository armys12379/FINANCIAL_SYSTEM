\# Legal Summary



\## Current Legal Framework



\### Accounting \& Reporting



TT 99/2025:

\- Defines financial reporting system (B01, B02, B03, B09)

\- Sets structure and presentation of financial statements



Luật 67/2025:

\- Core accounting law

\- Defines accounting principles and responsibilities

\- Requires full documentation and traceability



\---



\### Tax Regulations



NĐ 68/2026:

\- Defines tax treatment for individuals and household businesses (HKD)

\- Sets revenue thresholds for taxation

\- Specifies withholding obligations (TNCN)



NĐ 94/2026:

\- Defines operational conditions

\- Includes requirements for training activities and facilities



\---



\## Tax Principles



\### Corporate Income Tax (TNDN)

\- Expenses are deductible if:

&#x20; - directly related to business activities

&#x20; - supported by valid documentation

&#x20; - compliant with payment requirements



\### Value Added Tax (VAT)

\- Output VAT applies to taxable revenue

\- Input VAT is deductible if invoices are valid



\### Personal Income Tax (TNCN)

\- Withholding required depending on partner classification:

&#x20; - Individual

&#x20; - Household business (HKD)

&#x20; - Legal entity



\---



\## Accounting Principles



\- Revenue is recognized based on service delivery progress

\- Advance payments must be recorded as liabilities

\- Costs must match revenue (matching principle)

\- All transactions must have valid supporting documents



\---



\## Update Rule



\- New legal documents override previous ones

\- System must always comply with latest regulations
