\# System Context



\## System Name

Financial Accounting Management System



\## Scope

The system governs all financial and accounting activities:

\- Revenue

\- Cost

\- Partner transactions

\- Costing

\- Financial reporting

\- Internal control



\## Structure

The system contains 7 sections:



\- P1: General principles

\- P2: Revenue management

\- P3: Cost management

\- P4: Financial relationship with partners

\- P5: Costing and profitability

\- P6: Financial reporting

\- P7: Internal control and documentation



Total: 51 rules (Điều)



\## Core Principles



\- Compliance with accounting and tax regulations

\- Revenue-cost matching principle

\- Evidence-based accounting

\- Consistency across all sections

\- Auditability and traceability



\## Execution Model



\- Stateless full rebuild

\- No version labeling

\- Output replaces existing system in Notion

\- Each rebuild must be internally consistent



\## Dependencies



\- Revenue (P2) links with Cost (P3) and Costing (P5)

\- Cost (P3) affects Costing (P5) and Reporting (P6)

\- Partner (P4) affects Tax and Cost

\- Reporting (P6) depends on all sections

\- Control (P7) applies to all operations
