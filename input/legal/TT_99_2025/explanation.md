\# TT 99/2025/TT-BTC



\## Core Principles

\- Establish standardized financial reporting framework for enterprises

\- Ensure consistency, transparency, and comparability in financial statements

\- Apply accrual-based accounting and matching principle

\- Require full documentation and traceability of all accounting entries

\- Financial statements must reflect true and fair view of financial position and performance



\---



\## Tax Rules

\- Financial reports are the basis for tax declaration (TNDN, VAT, etc.)

\- Revenue and cost recognition directly affect taxable income

\- Any adjustment in accounting must align with tax regulations

\- Expenses must meet tax deductibility conditions to be recognized



\---



\## Accounting Impact

\- Define structure and format of financial statements:

&#x20; - Balance sheet (B01)

&#x20; - Income statement (B02)

&#x20; - Cash flow (B03)

&#x20; - Notes (B09)

\- Require consistent classification of revenue and cost

\- Mandate reconciliation between sub-ledgers and general ledger

\- Require proper allocation of cost and recognition of revenue



\---



\## Operational Impact

\- Require periodic reporting (monthly, quarterly, yearly)

\- Require approval workflow for financial statements

\- Require reconciliation before closing period

\- Require documentation retention and audit readiness

\- Establish responsibility of accounting roles (preparer, reviewer, approver)



\---



\## Affected Sections

\- P1: General principles (accounting rules foundation)

\- P2: Revenue (recognition rules)

\- P3: Cost (expense recognition and validation)

\- P5: Costing (allocation and matching)

\- P6: Financial reporting (core impact)

\- P7: Internal control (audit and compliance)



\---



\## Priority

Yes – This regulation defines core accounting standards and overrides internal rules when conflicts occur.
