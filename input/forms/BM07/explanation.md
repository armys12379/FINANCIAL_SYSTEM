\# BM-07 – Course Completion Acceptance



\## Purpose

Confirm completion of training course before recognizing revenue and cost.



\## Key Variables

\- Course ID

\- Completion date

\- Number of students completed

\- Training hours and distance

\- Instructor and partner data



\## Business Rules

\- Revenue cannot be recognized without BM-07 approval

\- Cost settlement cannot be processed without BM-07

\- Fuel and advance cannot be finalized without BM-07

\- Must meet training completion criteria



\## System Integration

\- P2: Revenue recognition trigger

\- P3: Cost settlement

\- P5: Cost calculation

\- P7: Control checkpoint



\## Notes

This is a mandatory control document (blocking condition).
