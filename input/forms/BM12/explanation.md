\# BM-12 – Exceptional Cost Approval



\## Purpose

Control and approve cost exceeding predefined norms.



\## Key Variables

\- Standard vs actual cost

\- Excess amount

\- Approval level



\## Business Rules

\- Required for any cost exceeding PL08 norm

\- Approval levels:

&#x20; - <1M: Accountant

&#x20; - 1–5M: Finance Director

&#x20; - >5M: CEO

\- Without BM-12 → cost cannot be recorded



\## System Integration

\- P3: Cost validation

\- P7: Internal control (core)



\## Notes

This enforces strong financial discipline.
