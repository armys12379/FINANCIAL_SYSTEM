\# BM-11 – Cost Transfer (TK 154 → TK 632)



\## Purpose

Transfer calculated cost to cost of goods sold.



\## Key Variables

\- Cost center (154A/B/C)

\- Cost transferred

\- Closing balance



\## Business Rules

\- Only completed cost is transferred

\- Must match BM-10 output

\- Accounting entry:

&#x20; - Debit 632

&#x20; - Credit 154



\## System Integration

\- P5: Costing

\- P6: Financial reporting

\- P7: Accounting control



\## Notes

Ensures correct profit calculation.
