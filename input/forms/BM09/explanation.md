\# BM-09 – Work-in-Progress Cost (TK 154)



\## Purpose

Determine cost of unfinished courses (WIP) at period end.



\## Key Variables

\- Opening WIP cost

\- Current period cost

\- Completed cost (transferred)

\- Ending WIP balance

\- % completion per course



\## Business Rules

\- WIP = Opening + Current – Completed

\- Only courses not completed are included

\- Training department must confirm WIP list

\- Final balance must match ledger TK 154



\## System Integration

\- P3: Cost tracking

\- P5: Cost calculation input

\- P6: Financial reporting (inventory/WIP)

\- P7: Control reconciliation



\## Notes

WIP must be validated before cost allocation and reporting.
