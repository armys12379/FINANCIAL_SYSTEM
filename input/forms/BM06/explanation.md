\# BM-06 – Fuel Issuance \& Vehicle Log



\## Purpose

Control fuel consumption and vehicle usage per training activity.



\## Key Variables

\- Fuel type

\- Consumption rate

\- Operating hours

\- Actual fuel issued

\- Distance travelled



\## Business Rules

\- Fuel issued must follow predefined norms

\- Excess usage requires BM-12 approval

\- Fuel advances for rented vehicles → TK 141

\- Vehicle logs must match fuel usage



\## System Integration

\- P3: Cost (fuel expense)

\- P5: Cost allocation

\- P7: Operational control



\## Notes

Fuel is a controlled cost with strict validation requirements.
