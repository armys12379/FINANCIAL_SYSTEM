\# Legal Impact Map



\## Accounting Laws

Affects:

\- P1: General principles

\- P2: Revenue

\- P3: Cost

\- P4: Partner

\- P5: Costing

\- P6: Reporting

\- P7: Internal control



\---



\## Tax Regulations (TNDN, VAT, TNCN)

Affects:

\- P2: Revenue (recognition, VAT)

\- P3: Cost (valid expenses)

\- P4: Partner (withholding obligations)

\- P5: Costing (tax-adjusted cost)

\- P6: Reporting (tax declarations)

\- P7: Control (compliance checks)



\---



\## Revenue Regulations

Affects:

\- P2: Revenue management

\- P5: Costing (revenue-cost linkage)

\- P6: Reporting



\---



\## Cost Regulations

Affects:

\- P3: Cost management

\- P5: Costing

\- P6: Reporting



\---



\## Partner / Contract Regulations

Affects:

\- P4: Partner structure and obligations

\- P3: Cost (outsourcing / contracts)

\- P2: Revenue (shared revenue models)



\---



\## Internal Control Regulations

Affects:

\- P7 only



\---



\## Operational / Industry Regulations

Affects:

\- P1: Definitions and scope

\- P2: Revenue model

\- P3: Cost structure
